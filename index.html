Connect your Pixl.js

  1. On the Pixl.js, open BLE File Transfer from the main menu.
  2. Click Connect device and pick Pixl.js from the list.
  3. Browse, drag files in to upload, and download or back up anything.

Needs Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android). Your files only travel between this browser and the device; nothing is uploaded to the internet.
